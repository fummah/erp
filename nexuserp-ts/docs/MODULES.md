# ERP Modules

## 1. Finance
General Ledger, AP, AR, Cash/Bank, budgeting foundation, multi-currency, tax, audit trail, reporting.

## 2. Procurement
Suppliers, requisitions foundation, purchase orders, GRNs, supplier invoicing foundation, approval workflow foundation.

## 3. Sales
Customers, quotations foundation, orders foundation, invoices, receipts foundation, credit/debit notes, statements/reporting foundation.

## 4. Inventory
Items, warehouses, movements, transfers/adjustments foundation, re-order levels, batch/serial fields, valuation foundation.

## 5. HR & Payroll
Employees, departments, leave/attendance/payroll data model, effective-dated statutory rules. Statutory formulas intentionally require verified current Zimbabwe rules before production activation.

## 6. CRM
Leads, opportunities, follow-ups/tasks foundation, customer history linkage.

## 7. Assets & Maintenance
Asset register, locations, depreciation fields, maintenance jobs/schedules foundation, disposal fields.

## 8. Compliance & Risk
Compliance obligations, risk register, audit items, calendars/exception foundation.

## 9. BI & Reporting
Dashboards and live GL-derived Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet endpoints.

## 10. Administration & Security
Users, roles, company switching, audit logs, branch/department models, platform admin.

## 11. Fiscalisation
Fiscal devices, device registration abstraction, fiscal days, sequential counters, invoice/credit/debit receipt model, mock provider, retry-ready statuses, FDMS request/response logs.

## 12. Platform SaaS
Tenants, legal companies, subscription plans, entitlements foundation, platform-admin APIs.
